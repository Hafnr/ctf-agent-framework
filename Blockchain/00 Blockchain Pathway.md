# Blockchain Pathway

Start with [[Blockchain/01 Smart Contracts and Solidity|Smart Contracts and Solidity]]. Preserve contract source, ABI, deployment bytecode, chain ID, RPC URL, addresses, deployment transaction, and the exact win condition.

Separate four layers: Solidity source, compiled EVM behavior, current on-chain state, and surrounding deployment/setup contracts. A source-level suspicion is not proven until a transaction demonstrates the expected state transition on the challenge chain.
