# OSINT Playbook

## Workflow

- [ ] Extract every clue exactly: spelling, timestamps, language, weather, signs, architecture, usernames, and image details.
- [ ] Separate direct evidence from inference.
- [ ] Reverse-search the whole image and distinctive crops.
- [ ] Search exact phrases in quotes and try language variants.
- [ ] Pivot consistently across usernames, domains, archives, commits, and metadata.
- [ ] Confirm identity or location with at least two independent clues.
- [ ] Save URLs, access dates, screenshots, and archived copies.

## Image/location questions

- Which side of the road, road markings, bollards, signs, utility poles?
- What script, language, domain suffix, phone format, or currency appears?
- What terrain, sun direction, vegetation, transit system, or architecture fits?
- Is metadata intact, and does it agree with visible evidence?

## Guardrails

- Stay within the competition's scope and rules.
- Do not contact real people or attempt account access unless the event explicitly requires and authorizes it.
- Treat social profiles and user-generated claims as leads, not proof.
