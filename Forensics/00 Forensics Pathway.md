## Evidence discipline

- hash originals and work from copies;
- record timezone and clock assumptions;
- distinguish filesystem metadata from application timestamps;
- preserve exact commands and tool versions;
- build a timeline before forming a narrative;
- label facts, interpretations, and unanswered questions separately.
