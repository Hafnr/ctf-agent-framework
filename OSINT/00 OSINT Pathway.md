# OSINT Pathway

## Learning order

1. [[OSINT/01 Search Identity and Web Archives|Search, identity, and web archives]]
2. [[OSINT/02 Image Geolocation and Chronolocation|Image geolocation and chronolocation]]
3. [[OSINT/03 Verification and Evidence|Verification and evidence]]

## Ethical scope

CTF OSINT should remain within the challenge scope. Do not contact real people, attempt account access, purchase data, or expose unrelated personal information.

## Investigation loop

```text
extract clues → generate hypotheses → search/pivot → verify independently
              → preserve evidence → state confidence and alternatives
```
